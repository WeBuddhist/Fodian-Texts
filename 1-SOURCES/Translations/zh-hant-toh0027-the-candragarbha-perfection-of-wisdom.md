# 聖般若波羅蜜多月藏大乘經

## 聖般若波羅蜜多月藏大乘經 ^1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-1]]

頂禮一切佛菩薩。 ^1-1

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-2]]

如是我聞：一時， ^1-2

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-3]]

世尊在王舍城靈鷲山，與無量無數比丘僧及菩薩眾俱。 ^1-3

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-4]]

爾時， ^1-4

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-5]]

月藏菩薩摩訶薩於會中， ^1-5

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-6]]

從座而起， ^1-6

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-7]]

白世尊言： ^1-7

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-8]]

「世尊！ ^1-8

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-9]]

諸菩薩應云何學般若波羅蜜多？」 ^1-9

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-10]]

世尊曰： ^1-10

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-11]]

「月藏！ ^1-11

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-12]]

一切法離實有， ^1-12

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-13]]

即般若波羅蜜多。 ^1-13

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-14]]

譬如月輪， ^1-14

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-15]]

無有分別， ^1-15

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-16]]

遶行四洲， ^1-16

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-17]]

盡除黑暗， ^1-17

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-18]]

如是菩薩摩訶薩行般若波羅蜜多， ^1-18

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-19]]

雖無分別， ^1-19

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-20]]

然以悲心周覆四倒眾生， ^1-20

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-21]]

自然能除一切煩惱， ^1-21

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-22]]

此亦以無分別故。」 ^1-22

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-23]]

月藏白曰： ^1-23

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-24]]

「世尊！ ^1-24

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-25]]

菩薩之般若波羅蜜多有幾何？」 ^1-25

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-26]]

世尊告曰： ^1-26

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-27]]

「善男子！ ^1-27

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-28]]

菩薩之般若波羅蜜多有二，謂：有漏、無漏。 ^1-28

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-29]]

善男子！ ^1-29

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-30]]

此中有漏般若者， ^1-30

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-31]]

謂於信解行地位， ^1-31

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-32]]

有能、所二取分別  ，能斷彼者， ^1-32

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-33]]

是為有漏般若波羅蜜多。 ^1-33

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-34]]

善男子！ ^1-34

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-35]]

此中無漏般若波羅蜜多者， ^1-35

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-36]]

謂見道之無分別智， ^1-36

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-37]]

以此智離諸分別而無分別故。 ^1-37

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-38]]

善男子！ ^1-38

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-39]]

此亦般若波羅蜜多， ^1-39

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-40]]

於彼邊際，或此邊際，俱無所住故。 ^1-40

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-41]]

當知般若者， ^1-41

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-42]]

從色乃至一切種智無自性故， ^1-42

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-43]]

般若波羅蜜多無自性； ^1-43

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-44]]

當知色等諸法 ， ^1-44

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-45]]

於三時不可得， ^1-45

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-46]]

無縛無解。 ^1-46

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-47]]

善男子！ ^1-47

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-48]]

一切法平等故， ^1-48

般若波羅蜜多亦平等； ^1-49

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-50]]

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-51]]

般若波羅蜜多亦不滅； ^1-51

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-52]]

一切法無相故， ^1-52

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-53]]

般若波羅蜜多亦無相； ^1-53

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-56]]

般若波羅蜜多亦不斷； ^1-57

一切法不來故， ^1-58

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0027-the-candragarbha-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-59]]

般若波羅蜜多亦不來； ^1-59

一切法不去故， ^1-60

般若波羅蜜多亦不去； ^1-61
