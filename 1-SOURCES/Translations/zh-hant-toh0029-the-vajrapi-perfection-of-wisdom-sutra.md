# 聖般若波羅蜜多金剛手大乘經

## 聖般若波羅蜜多金剛手大乘經 ^1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-1]]

頂禮一切佛菩薩。 ^1-1

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-2]]

如是我聞： ^1-2

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-3]]

一時， ^1-3

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-4]]

世尊在王舍城。 ^1-4

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-5]]

爾時金剛手菩薩在眷屬中，從座而起， ^1-5

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-6]]

白世尊言： ^1-6

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-7]]

「世尊！ ^1-7

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-8]]

菩薩應云何學般若波羅蜜多？」 ^1-8

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-9]]

世尊告曰： ^1-9

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-10]]

「金剛手！ ^1-10

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-11]]

菩薩應如是學般若波羅蜜多，謂： ^1-11

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-12]]

應以一切法無生， ^1-12

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-13]]

學般若波羅蜜多； ^1-13

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-14]]

復次， ^1-14

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-15]]

應以色等諸法無自性、 ^1-15

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-16]]

無相、 ^1-16

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-17]]

離實有、 ^1-17

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-18]]

不可得， ^1-18

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-19]]

學般若波羅蜜多； ^1-19

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-20]]

應以內空、 ^1-20

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-21]]

無法空故， ^1-21

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-22]]

學般若波羅蜜多。 ^1-22

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-23]]

金剛手！ ^1-23

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-24]]

般若波羅蜜多是三世諸佛之母， ^1-24

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-25]]

其大明咒句如是： ^1-25

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-26]]

嗡．牟尼．牟尼．摩訶牟那耶．莎訶。Om muni muni mahāmunaye svāhā. ^1-26

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-27]]

「若人受持此般若波羅蜜多， ^1-27

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-28]]

即受持三世諸佛之法， ^1-28

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-29]]

彼能自憶宿命， ^1-29

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-30]]

斷諸無暇， ^1-30

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-31]]

得無量福果， ^1-31

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-32]]

常與諸佛世尊同共止住， ^1-32

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-33]]

具菩提心。」 ^1-33

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-34]]

世尊說是語已， ^1-34

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-35]]

金剛手菩薩及天、人、非天、乾闥婆等世間大眾，皆大歡喜， ^1-35

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-36]]

於世尊所說，稱揚讚歎。 ^1-36

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0029-the-vajrapi-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-37]]

般若波羅蜜多金剛手大乘經 ^1-37
