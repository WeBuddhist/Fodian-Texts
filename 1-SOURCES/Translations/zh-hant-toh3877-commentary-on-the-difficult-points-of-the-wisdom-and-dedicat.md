# 入菩薩行智慧品和迴向品難語釋

## 入菩薩行智慧品和迴向品難語釋 ^1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-1]]

入菩薩行智慧品和迴向品難語釋 ^1-1

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-2]]

彼補特伽羅，心平等而住，為了證得殊勝果位，當需生起智慧，故今說第八品[^0]。 ^1-2

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-3]]

其中，問：「當依於什麼而生起？」 ^1-3

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-4]]

謂在靜慮品中，住於奢摩他資糧， ^1-4

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-5]]

預先修習奢摩他。 問：「這些一切又為了什麼而準備？」 ^1-5

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-6]]

為令於自、他心續上生起而準備。 ^1-6

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-7]]

前靜慮品說： ^1-7

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-8]]

「有止諸勝觀， ^1-8

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-9]]

能滅諸煩惱， ^1-9

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-10]]

知已先求止。」 其義謂：如所言準備已， ^1-10

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-11]]

自、他心續希求涅槃及安樂，因此，為獲得它，當生起智慧！ ^1-11

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-12]]

其中所說：「涅槃」是自利， ^1-12

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-13]]

安樂是他利； ^1-13

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-14]]

或涅槃是斷德， ^1-14

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-15]]

安樂是妙智； ^1-15

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-16]]

另或，涅槃是決定勝， ^1-16

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-17]]

安樂是增上生。 ^1-17

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-18]]

如果這麼想：「如是有靜慮者，為了獲得涅槃及安樂， 當生起何種智慧？」 ^1-18

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-19]]

此說明當生起緣二諦境的智慧！ ^1-19

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-20]]

「說」[^1]，謂「由如來（說）」是補充說明語。 ^1-20

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-21]]

如果這麼想：「他們各自之相是怎樣的呢？」 ^1-21

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-22]]

對於所說「勝義非心境」， ^1-22

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-23]]

其中所說「心」，僅以其為例而已，亦當了知非聲之境。 ^1-23

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-24]]

因為經中說：「勝義諦者，尚無心行，況諸文字。」 ^1-24

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-25]]

又說：「天子！ 勝義諦者，正超一切名言， ^1-25

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-26]]

不生不滅，遠離能詮、所詮，遠離能知、所知！」 ^1-26

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-27]]

如果這麼想：「然而，彼勝義為何不是心的行境？」 ^1-27

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-28]]

勝義是如實正理， ^1-28

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-29]]

若以正理觀察，任何事皆不成立， ^1-29

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-30]]

如經說 「若以心審察，無依他無計」等。 ^1-30

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-31]]

聖者龍樹也說： ^1-31

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-32]]

「隨作一切說， ^1-32

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-33]]

當知自性空！ ^1-33

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-34]]

所說『空』亦空， ^1-34

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-35]]

如是無非空。」 ^1-35

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-36]]

《二諦》也說 ^1-36

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-37]]

「真實性故彼非空（、非不空）」等。不管怎樣，皆不成立故， ^1-37

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-38]]

若以正理觀察，無論如何，心與聲皆不以其為境。 ^1-38

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-39]]

或者，所說「勝義」，是於依他起相，唯斷除增益戲論而已， ^1-39

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-40]]

故無論如何，心與聲皆不以其為境， ^1-40

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-41]]

（唯斷除增益戲論之）唯遮非任何（心聲之境）故。 ^1-41

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-42]]

與其相反，所說「心與聲是世俗」，如《宣說聖二諦》說： ^1-42

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-43]]

「天子！勝義之中，勝義之諦，若成身語意對境之體， 則不入所說『勝義諦』數； ^1-43

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-44]]

彼唯成世俗諦！ ^1-44

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-45]]

天子！勝義之中，勝義之諦， ^1-45

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-46]]

亦正超出，具一切種最勝一切智智之境！」 ^1-46

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-47]]

如《聖無盡慧菩薩所示經》也說： ^1-47

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-48]]

「云何世俗諦？ ^1-48

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-49]]

盡所有世間名言施設、文字、語言、名號所示。」 ^1-49

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-50]]

世俗者，是如所顯現故。作如上的思維！ ^1-50

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-51]]

詳細當從其他中觀論典中了知故，此處不作廣說。 ^1-51

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-52]]

今祛除對於事物本性的邪分別，而從此方面，為略示觀察事物本性故， ^1-52

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-53]]

於剛才宣說事物本性，瑜伽士，及隨順凡庸故為凡庸的經部師等諸世間人，依次以無二之理及二相之理觀見。 ^1-53

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-54]]

其中，瑜伽行者能破斥凡庸世間人的觀點， ^1-54

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-55]]

而超勝凡庸世間人的瑜伽士觀點，則以較彼（瑜伽士）更勝的中觀師見的特法，能破斥之，故斷言「因此，當觀察相！」 ^1-55

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-56]]

今首先就詳細解說它而言， ^1-56

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-57]]

問：「然凡庸世間所許是什麼？ ^1-57

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-58]]

瑜伽行者如何破彼？」 ^1-58

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-59]]

凡庸世間的經部師等， ^1-59

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-60]]

「見實法」謂於色等； ^1-60

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-61]]

「為真實」，謂於如所顯現的真實外境。 ^1-61

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-62]]

「如幻化」，謂如那般顯現。 ^1-62

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-63]]

問：「如何諍論？」 ^1-63

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-64]]

謂作是說： ^1-64

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-65]]

「色等是由戲論習氣成熟之力，錯亂為現前性，從而顯現彼，並不以量成立， ^1-65

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-66]]

以無成立彼的正理故， 並且 ^1-66

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-67]]

以一多等量能妨害故。 ^1-67

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-68]]

如於不淨，執著為淨。」 ^1-68

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-69]]

問：「然而，如果這樣，則違背言教《聖義決定法數》中所說： 『云何色？凡色皆可，彼一切皆是四大種，及以四大種為因。』」 ^1-69

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-70]]

是宣說：「如是所說，是為了引導應當調伏的諸士夫而說，而非實有故而說， ^1-70

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-71]]

故不相違。」 ^1-71

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-72]]

問：「可見到哪般的比喻嗎？」 ^1-72

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-73]]

如《聖般若波羅蜜多經》說： ^1-73

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-74]]

「須菩提！ ^1-74

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-75]]

譬如極寂靜如來未見一位可授記有情，遂變現一化身， ^1-75

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-76]]

而入涅槃。 ^1-76

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-77]]

其後，彼化身作佛事業， ^1-77

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-78]]

於諸菩薩，授記無上菩提後， ^1-78

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-79]]

而般涅槃。」以如是宣說之理，為令於所化機心續上生起福德， ^1-79

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-80]]

顯示猶如變現的幻化。 ^1-80

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-81]]

如是宣說了瑜伽行師如何破除凡庸世間人的所許情形後， ^1-81

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-82]]

今為宣說中觀師如何破除瑜伽行師的所許故， ^1-82

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-83]]

如果說：「所說『有情』，若此等皆是勝義之中無自性， ^1-83

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-84]]

則如幻般，死後應不再生，有此過失，然而並非如是， ^1-84

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-85]]

生故勝義有。」 ^1-85

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-86]]

由於一切皆是世俗，然一切世俗不決定輪回故， ^1-86

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-87]]

其義謂：若某時，未生起對治智慧故，猶未遮遣惑業因的時候，則不會遮遣苦蘊果，復當輪回； ^1-87

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-88]]

而若某時，已生起對治，破逆品惑業， ^1-88

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-89]]

不生苦蘊果， ^1-89

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-90]]

當不輪回，爾時，生滅的相雖是世俗，但不是輪回，故不決定。 ^1-90

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-91]]

又瑜伽行師說： ^1-91

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh3877-commentary-on-the-difficult-points-of-the-wisdom-and-dedicat.md#^1-92]]
