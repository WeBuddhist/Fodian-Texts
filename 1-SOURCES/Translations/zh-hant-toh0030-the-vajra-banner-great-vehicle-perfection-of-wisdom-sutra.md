# 聖般若波羅蜜多金剛幢大乘經

## 聖般若波羅蜜多金剛幢大乘經 ^1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-1]]

頂禮一切佛菩薩。 ^1-1

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-2]]

如是我聞：一時， ^1-2

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-3]]

世尊在王舍城， ^1-3

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-4]]

與一切眷屬大眾俱。 ^1-4

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-5]]

爾時，金剛幢菩薩在會中，從座而起， ^1-5

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-6]]

白世尊言： ^1-6

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-7]]

「世尊！具足何法，是般若波羅蜜多？」 ^1-7

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-8]]

世尊告曰： ^1-8

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-9]]

「金剛幢！當具足四法。 ^1-9

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-10]]

云何為四？ ^1-10

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-11]]

謂： ^1-11

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-12]]

應修諸法性空； ^1-12

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-13]]

應知諸行無常、知苦； ^1-13

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-14]]

應修具足離諸戲論法性； ^1-14

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-15]]

應於一切中，修無分別，知一切法，皆不可得。」 ^1-15

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-16]]

「復次，若具四法，是般若生因。 ^1-16

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-17]]

云何為四？ ^1-17

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-18]]

初者、依止說法補特伽羅， ^1-18

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-19]]

二者、聞其［所說］正法， ^1-19

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-20]]

三者、［既聞所說，］趣入其義，篤行思惟， ^1-20

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-21]]

四者、復能立志如理為他宣說。 ^1-21

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-22]]

是為般若生因。」 ^1-22

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-23]]

「復次，善男子！諸菩薩應知： ^1-23

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-24]]

一切法自性清淨，故其性空、 ^1-24

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-25]]

無我、 ^1-25

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-26]]

無實， ^1-26

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-27]]

本性無相， ^1-27

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-28]]

無縛無解， ^1-28

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-29]]

無隱無顯， ^1-29

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-30]]

是故諸菩薩應學般若波羅蜜多， ^1-30

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-31]]

應以一切法無生無住理趣，學般若波羅蜜多。」 ^1-31

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-32]]

世尊告言： ^1-32

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-33]]

「應修無實有， ^1-33

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-34]]

信般若生因， ^1-34

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-35]]

應離一切相，是學般若義。」 ^1-35

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-36]]

世尊說是語已， ^1-36

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-37]]

金剛幢菩薩及天、人、非天、乾闥婆等世間［大眾］，皆大歡喜， ^1-37

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-38]]

於世尊所說，稱揚讚歎。 ^1-38

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0030-the-vajra-banner-great-vehicle-perfection-of-wisdom-sutra.md#^1-39]]

聖般若波羅蜜多金剛幢大乘經 ^1-39
