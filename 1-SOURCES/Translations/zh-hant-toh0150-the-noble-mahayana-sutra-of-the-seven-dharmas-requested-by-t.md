## 聖觀自在菩薩所問七法大乘經 ^1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-2]]

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-6]]

并與眾多菩薩摩訶薩大眾俱。 ^1-6

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-7]]

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-8]]

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-9]]

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-10]]

向於佛所， ^1-10

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-11]]

合掌禮敬， ^1-11

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-12]]

白世尊言： ^1-12

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-13]]

「世尊！ ^1-13

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-14]]

菩薩初發菩提心已， ^1-14

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-15]]

當學幾法？。」 ^1-15

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-16]]

「善男子！菩薩初發菩提心已， ^1-17

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-18]]

應學七法。 ^1-18

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-19]]

云何為七？ ^1-19

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-20]]

謂： ^1-20

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-21]]

雖起分別， ^1-21

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-22]]

亦不應依止貪欲， ^1-22

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-23]]

何況二根交合； ^1-23

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-24]]

乃至夢中， ^1-24

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-25]]

亦不應依不善知識； ^1-25

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-26]]

應如禽鳥， ^1-26

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-27]]

心無攝取； ^1-27

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-28]]

應以善巧方便慧故， ^1-28

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-29]]

無有我慢，亦無我執； ^1-29

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-30]]

有實無實，皆悉應斷， ^1-30

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-31]]

於空解脫，堅固具足； ^1-31

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-32]]

應以［了悟諸法］非諦實之分別智， ^1-32

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-33]]

於輪迴幻夢， ^1-33

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-34]]

無所好樂； ^1-34

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-35]]

不謗因果。 ^1-35

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-36]]

善男子！菩薩初發菩提心， ^1-36

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-37]]

應學如是七法。」 ^1-37

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-38]]

世尊說是語已， ^1-38

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-39]]

聖觀自在菩薩及彼諸比丘、菩薩，皆大歡喜， ^1-39

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-40]]

於世尊所說，稱揚讚歎。 ^1-40

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-41]]

聖觀自在菩薩所問七法大乘經 ^1-41

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0150-the-noble-mahayana-sutra-of-the-seven-dharmas-requested-by-t.md#^1-43]]
