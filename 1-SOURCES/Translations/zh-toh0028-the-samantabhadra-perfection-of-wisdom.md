# 聖般若波羅蜜多普賢大乘經

## 聖般若波羅蜜多普賢大乘經 ^1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0028-the-samantabhadra-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-1]]

頂禮一切佛菩薩。 ^1-1

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0028-the-samantabhadra-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-2]]

如是我聞：一時， ^1-2

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0028-the-samantabhadra-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-3]]

世尊在摩竭陀國阿蘭若法菩提場， ^1-3

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0028-the-samantabhadra-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-4]]

與從十方無量無數佛剎來會佛剎微塵數菩薩俱， ^1-4

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0028-the-samantabhadra-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-5]]

此諸大眾於普賢願，自在具足，住普賢行。 ^1-5

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0028-the-samantabhadra-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-6]]

爾時普賢菩薩等入三昧，名「示顯諸法無分別」。 ^1-6

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0028-the-samantabhadra-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-7]]

以是三昧力，等佛剎微塵數一切世界皆悉震動。 ^1-7

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0028-the-samantabhadra-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-8]]

復次，世尊摩普賢菩薩頂， ^1-8

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0028-the-samantabhadra-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-9]]

既摩頂已，一切佛土，乃出遍動。 ^1-9

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0028-the-samantabhadra-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-10]]

而諸天人，普共一音，以偈讚曰： ^1-10

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0028-the-samantabhadra-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-11]]

「世燈人天無能喻，功德如海到彼岸， ^1-11

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0028-the-samantabhadra-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-12]]

祈請商主作救護，救度眾生出有海。」 ^1-12

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0028-the-samantabhadra-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-13]]

復次，普賢菩薩白世尊言： ^1-13

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0028-the-samantabhadra-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-14]]

「世尊！［此等］所應學義，謂相似般若及真正般若，應云何學？」 ^1-14

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0028-the-samantabhadra-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-15]]

世尊告曰： ^1-15

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0028-the-samantabhadra-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-16]]

「普賢！世間資糧道行者之慧，是為相似般若； ^1-16

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0028-the-samantabhadra-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-17]]

從於初地以上之慧，是為真正般若。」 ^1-17

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0028-the-samantabhadra-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-18]]

世尊說是語已， ^1-18

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0028-the-samantabhadra-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-19]]

普賢菩薩及天、人等世間［大眾］皆大歡喜， ^1-19

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0028-the-samantabhadra-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-20]]

於世尊所說，稱揚讚歎。 ^1-20

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0028-the-samantabhadra-perfection-of-wisdom.md#^1-21]]

聖般若波羅蜜多普賢大乘經 ^1-21
