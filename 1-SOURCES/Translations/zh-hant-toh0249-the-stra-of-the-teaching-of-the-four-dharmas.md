一時， ^1-3

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-5]]

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-8]]

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-9]]

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-10]]

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-11]]

「云何為四？ ^1-11

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-12]]

破惡正行、 ^1-13

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-14]]

還淨力、 ^1-15

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-16]]

依止力。 ^1-16

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-17]]

「此中，破惡正行者，謂： ^1-17

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-18]]

既行不善業已， ^1-18

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-19]]

於彼多生悔心。 ^1-19

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-20]]

「此中，對治正行者，謂： ^1-20

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-21]]

既行不善業已， ^1-21

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-22]]

而於善業，能發精勤。 ^1-22

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-23]]

「此中，還淨力者，謂： ^1-23

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-24]]

以正受律儀故， ^1-24

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-25]]

得不復作[不善業]之律儀。 ^1-25

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-26]]

「此中，依止力者，謂： ^1-26

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-27]]

歸依佛法僧、 ^1-27

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-28]]

不捨菩提心。 ^1-28

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-29]]

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-30]]

「彌勒！菩薩摩訶薩若具是等四法， ^1-31

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-32]]

則能勝伏已作已集之惡。 ^1-32

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-33]]

菩薩摩訶薩應常披讀、 ^1-33

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-34]]

諷誦、 ^1-34

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-35]]

思惟、 ^1-35

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-36]]

修習是經， ^1-36

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-37]]

數數修持。 ^1-37

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-38]]

以是之故，則[雖]作惡， ^1-38

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0249-the-stra-of-the-teaching-of-the-four-dharmas.md#^1-39]]
