## 三學經 ^1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0282-sutra-on-the-threefold-training.md#^1-1]]

頂禮一切佛菩薩。 ^1-1

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0282-sutra-on-the-threefold-training.md#^1-2]]

如是我聞： ^1-2

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0282-sutra-on-the-threefold-training.md#^1-3]]

一時， ^1-3

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0282-sutra-on-the-threefold-training.md#^1-4]]

世尊在跋耆國， ^1-4

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0282-sutra-on-the-threefold-training.md#^1-5]]

遊行至竹林聚落 ，於竹林聚落北娑羅樹林中， ^1-5

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0282-sutra-on-the-threefold-training.md#^1-6]]

與大比丘僧俱。 ^1-6

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0282-sutra-on-the-threefold-training.md#^1-7]]

爾時，世尊告眾比丘： ^1-7

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0282-sutra-on-the-threefold-training.md#^1-8]]

「諸比丘！此等是戒， ^1-8

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0282-sutra-on-the-threefold-training.md#^1-9]]

此是定， ^1-9

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0282-sutra-on-the-threefold-training.md#^1-10]]

此是慧。 ^1-10

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0282-sutra-on-the-threefold-training.md#^1-11]]

諸比丘！若能習戒， ^1-11

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0282-sutra-on-the-threefold-training.md#^1-12]]

將久住定； ^1-12

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0282-sutra-on-the-threefold-training.md#^1-13]]

若能習定， ^1-13

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0282-sutra-on-the-threefold-training.md#^1-14]]

當能得慧； ^1-14

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0282-sutra-on-the-threefold-training.md#^1-15]]

若能習慧， ^1-15

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0282-sutra-on-the-threefold-training.md#^1-16]]

當能令心於貪、瞋、癡得正解脫。 ^1-16

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0282-sutra-on-the-threefold-training.md#^1-17]]

如是心正解脫之聖聲聞， ^1-17

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0282-sutra-on-the-threefold-training.md#^1-18]]

我漏已盡， ^1-18

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0282-sutra-on-the-threefold-training.md#^1-19]]

梵行已立， ^1-19

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0282-sutra-on-the-threefold-training.md#^1-20]]

所作已辦， ^1-20

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0282-sutra-on-the-threefold-training.md#^1-21]]

自知不受後有 。」 ^1-21

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0282-sutra-on-the-threefold-training.md#^1-22]]

世尊說是語已， ^1-22

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0282-sutra-on-the-threefold-training.md#^1-23]]

彼諸比丘皆大歡喜， ^1-23

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0282-sutra-on-the-threefold-training.md#^1-24]]

於世尊所說稱揚讚歎。 ^1-24
