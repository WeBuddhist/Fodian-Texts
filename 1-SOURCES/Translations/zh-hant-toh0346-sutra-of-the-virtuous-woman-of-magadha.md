# 摩竭陀賢女經

## 摩竭陀賢女經 ^1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-1]]

頂禮一切佛菩薩。 ^1-1

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-2]]

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-3]]

彼時，有給孤獨長者女，名摩竭陀賢，具足信心，稟性佳妙，意樂良善，兼利自他，住於舍衛城。 ^1-3

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-4]]

於時福增城中， ^1-4

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-5]]

別有商主， ^1-5

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-7]]

親近外道， ^1-7

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-8]]

深信諸尼犍子。 ^1-8

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-9]]

於時世尊現大神變， ^1-9

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-10]]

諸外道皆住於邊城， ^1-10

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-11]]

或於作賢城， ^1-11

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-12]]

或於遨哩迦境內， ^1-12

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-13]]

或住福增城。 ^1-13

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-14]]

彼時 ^1-14

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-15]]

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-16]]

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-18]]

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-19]]

形色妙好， ^1-19

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-20]]

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-21]]

如諸賢士。」 ^1-21

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-22]]

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-32]]

返於福增城， ^1-33

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-34]]

稟報其父， ^1-34

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-35]]

旋往提親。 ^1-35

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-36]]

給孤獨長者遂來諮稟於佛， ^1-36

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-37]]

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-38]]

「摩竭陀賢若往福增城，未來某時， ^1-38

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-40]]

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-41]]

攜其女往福增城。 ^1-41

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-42]]

未久， ^1-42

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-43]]

諸尼犍子群至該家受食， ^1-43

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-44]]

其姥語摩竭陀賢曰： ^1-44

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-45]]

「今日於家設供以款諸應供者， ^1-45

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-46]]

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-47]]

摩竭陀賢聞言， ^1-47

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-48]]

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-49]]

「此定為尊者舍利子、大目犍連等諸大聲聞至焉。」 ^1-49

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-50]]

心極歡喜， ^1-50

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-53]]

摩竭陀賢見已，即生羞惡， ^1-53

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-54]]

「新婦何故失望若此？」 ^1-56

彼女答曰： ^1-57

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-58]]

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0346-sutra-of-the-virtuous-woman-of-magadha.md#^1-70]]

戒律清淨智深湛， ^1-70
