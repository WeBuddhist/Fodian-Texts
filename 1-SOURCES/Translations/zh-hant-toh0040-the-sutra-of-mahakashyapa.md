# 大迦葉經

## 大迦葉經 ^1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-1]]

敬禮聖三寶。 ^1-1

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-2]]

如是我聞：一時， ^1-2

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-3]]

［佛］在王舍城迦蘭陀竹林精舍。 ^1-3

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-4]]

爾時，具壽大迦葉在畢波羅窟， ^1-4

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-5]]

為重病所逼苦。 ^1-5

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-6]]

世尊於初夜時， ^1-6

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-7]]

［入諸法］分別妙觀而住。 ^1-7

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-8]]

是後復往具壽大迦葉所，至已，敷座而坐。 ^1-8

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-9]]

坐已，世尊告具壽大迦葉曰： ^1-9

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-10]]

「迦葉！汝猶欲［飲］食不？ ^1-10

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-11]]

受苦若何？ ^1-11

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-12]]

云何得瘳？ ^1-12

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-13]]

若依於療病之法，非不能瘳也。」 ^1-13

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-14]]

具壽大迦葉乃白［佛］言： ^1-14

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-15]]

「大德 ！我於飲食，已無愛染， ^1-15

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-16]]

今為重病所逼， ^1-16

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-17]]

痊濟難料， ^1-17

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-18]]

願賜療癒之方。」 ^1-18

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-19]]

［世尊告曰］： ^1-19

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-20]]

「我今當［為汝］善說此七菩提分。 ^1-20

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-21]]

若多修習，能得神通、證菩提、趣涅槃。 ^1-21

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-22]]

云何為七？ ^1-22

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-23]]

迦葉！我說此正念菩提分是真實菩提分， ^1-23

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-24]]

若多修習，能得神通、證菩提、趣涅槃； ^1-24

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-25]]

復次，迦葉！我說擇法是真實菩提分， ^1-25

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-26]]

若多修習，能得神通、證菩提、趣涅槃； ^1-26

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-27]]

復次，迦葉！我說精進是真實菩提分， ^1-27

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-28]]

若多修習，能得神通、證菩提、趣涅槃； ^1-28

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-29]]

復次，迦葉！我說喜是真實菩提分， ^1-29

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-30]]

若多修習，能得神通、證菩提、趣涅槃； ^1-30

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-31]]

復次，迦葉！我說輕安是真實菩提分， ^1-31

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-32]]

若多修習，能得神通、證菩提、趣涅槃； ^1-32

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-33]]

復次，迦葉！我說定是真實菩提分， ^1-33

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-34]]

若多修習，能得神通、證菩提、趣涅槃； ^1-34

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-35]]

復次，迦葉！我說捨是真實菩提分， ^1-35

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-36]]

若多修習，能得神通、證菩提、趣涅槃。 ^1-36

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-37]]

迦葉！我今說此七菩提分， ^1-37

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-38]]

若多修習，能得神通、證菩提、趣涅槃。 ^1-38

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-39]]

於世尊如來所說菩提分，汝當修學。」 ^1-39

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-40]]

世尊說是語已， ^1-40

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-41]]

具壽大迦葉於世尊所說，稱揚讚歎， ^1-41

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-42]]

其病即瘳。 ^1-42

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-43]]

具壽大迦葉［於座］乃起， ^1-43

![[1-SOURCES/Text/bo-toh0040-the-sutra-of-mahakashyapa.md#^1-44]]

具壽大迦葉病瘳矣。 ^1-44
